Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice applies to messages sent through petermarcfarrugia.com.

What the form collects

The form collects the name, email address and message entered by the sender. Formspree may also process the ordinary technical data needed to transmit a web form, such as an IP address, browser information and the date and time of submission.

Where a submission goes

Submissions are transmitted to Formspree. Formspree forwards the message to pytfarrugia@gmail.com and makes a copy available in its form dashboard. The receiving email is stored in the associated Gmail account until it is deleted.

Why the information is used

General enquiries are handled on the basis of the legitimate interest in reading and answering messages addressed to this site. An enquiry about psychotherapy may also be handled because the sender has asked for steps to be taken before a possible professional agreement.

Retention

Ordinary enquiries are deleted from Formspree and the receiving mailbox within 30 days after a reply has been given, or after a decision has been made not to proceed. Where an enquiry leads to a professional relationship, any information that must be retained is handled separately under the applicable professional and legal duties.

Deletion and access

To ask what information is held, correct it or request deletion, email pytfarrugia@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. A deletion request will be honoured unless retention is required by law or by an established professional duty.

Clinical and safeguarding information

The form and ordinary email are not confidential or secure clinical channels. Do not use either to disclose detailed clinical information, to seek emergency help, or to report a safeguarding concern.

Service provider

Formspree acts as the form-processing service. Its own privacy and security terms also apply to the transmission and temporary storage of submissions.
