Record of Nighttime

Selected drawings in graphite, ink and silverpoint, curated by Melanie Erixon at il-Kamra ta’ Fuq, Mqabba.

Pietru is the Maltese form of Peter and the name used by his grandparents. The drawings and exhibitions are signed Pietru Farrugia: not as a second identity, but as the name under which the visual work has taken form.

The exhibition gathers work made through graphite, ink, silverpoint and prepared papers. Some sheets are worked with rabbit-skin glue, clay or bone ash so that the ground changes with touch and light.
