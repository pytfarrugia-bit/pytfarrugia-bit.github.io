Practice

Individual psychotherapy

Peter offers individual psychotherapy to adults and older adolescents who may be dealing with anxiety, trauma, abuse, relationship difficulties, grief, shame or a period of change. You do not need a diagnosis or a settled explanation of what is wrong before making contact.

His approach is Gestalt psychotherapy informed by trauma theory. Sessions attend to what is happening now: how experience is felt in the body, how contact with other people is made or interrupted, and what patterns become visible in the room. Work is available in English or Maltese.

Sessions last 50 minutes. They are offered in person at O’Hea Pharmacy, 115 Triq Manoel De Vilhena, Gżira GZR 1017, or online by Zoom. Peter is currently accepting a limited number of new clients. The fee is €40 per session; in-person appointments also carry a €5 clinic fee. A limited concession may sometimes be agreed where the full fee would prevent someone from beginning.

Peter holds Malta psychotherapy warrant no. 314. “Warranted” means that he is statutorily authorised to practise under Malta’s Psychotherapy Profession Act; for UK readers, it is the Maltese form of admission to the national professional register. He is not currently registered with a UK professional regulator.

To ask about a first appointment, email pytfarrugia@gmail.com. A first meeting can be used to decide whether the work feels suitable.
