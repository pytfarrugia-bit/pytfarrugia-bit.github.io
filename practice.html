Gestalt psychotherapy in Malta and online

Psychotherapy after traumatic experience

I offer individual psychotherapy to adults recovering from traumatic or overwhelming experiences, in English or Maltese, in person in Malta or online.

How trauma may appear

Trauma does not always remain as a clear memory of a past event. It may appear as intrusive memories, bodily distress, difficulty feeling safe, anxiety or depressive tendencies, shame, withdrawal, or strain in relationships. Sometimes a person knows only that familiar ways of coping no longer hold.

How I work

My approach is Gestalt and trauma-informed. I attend to how experience appears in the body, in relationships, and in the present encounter. The work is not to force disclosure or hurry towards resolution, but to find a pace at which difficult experience can be approached without becoming overwhelming, and from which different responses may gradually become possible.

Religion and spiritual life

Religious belief, spiritual experience, doubt, or conflict may be explored when they matter to the client. No particular belief is assumed, and therapy is not spiritual direction or religious formation.
