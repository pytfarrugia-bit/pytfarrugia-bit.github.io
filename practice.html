Gestalt psychotherapy in Malta and online

Psychotherapy attentive to trauma, relationship, meaning and the ways experience is carried in the body.

I offer individual psychotherapy to adults and older adolescents, in English or Maltese. In-person and online sessions are available, and I am currently accepting enquiries.

Who I work with

People come to therapy for many reasons: anxiety, depression, trauma, shame, grief, difficulties in relationships, spiritual conflict, or a sense that familiar ways of coping no longer work. A person does not need to arrive with a diagnosis or a complete explanation of what is wrong.

How I work

My approach is Gestalt and trauma-informed. I pay attention not only to what is said, but to how experience appears in the body, in relationships and in the immediate encounter between therapist and client. Therapy may involve exploring repeated patterns, present bodily responses, unfinished experiences and the meanings formed around what has happened.

I do not assume that distress can be reduced to a symptom or resolved by explanation alone. The pace of the work is considered carefully, particularly where trauma, shame or experiences of being overwhelmed are involved.

Religion and spiritual life

Religious belief, spiritual experience, doubt and conflict may be brought into therapy when they matter to the client. They are approached as part of the person’s experience, without requiring any particular belief or practice. Therapy is not religious direction, and theological ideas are not imposed upon the work.

Professional standing

I am a warranted Gestalt psychotherapist in Malta. My work is undertaken within professional supervision and the ethical and legal requirements attached to the psychotherapy profession. Alongside clinical practice, I mentor psychotherapy students during placement and work as a visiting lecturer, trainer and workshop facilitator.

Beginning

An initial session allows us to consider what brings you to therapy, what you hope might change and whether working together seems appropriate. There is no obligation to continue after that first meeting. To enquire, use the contact form and indicate whether you are seeking in-person or online sessions and the times at which you are generally available.

Please note: the contact form is not monitored continuously and cannot provide emergency or crisis support.
