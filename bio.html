Bio

Peter Marc Farrugia

Psychotherapist, theological researcher and writer from Malta.

Peter Marc Farrugia is a warranted Gestalt psychotherapist and a doctoral researcher in theology at St Mary’s University, London. His work brings Christian theology into conversation with trauma, safeguarding, memory and symbolic practice. His current research concerns ecclesial time, judgement, memory and the limits of closure after harm. He studied Divinity at the University of Cambridge and has further postgraduate training in conflict analysis and psychotherapy.

Pietru is the Maltese form of Peter and the name his grandparents used. Drawings and exhibitions are signed with it.

He practises as a psychotherapist, and works as a visiting lecturer, trainer and workshop facilitator.

Doctoral research at St Mary's University, Twickenham, on Holy Saturday and the limits of completion, and on the form of attendance that Mary keeps at the sealed place.

Alongside academic writing and clinical practice, he develops devotional, visual and musical projects concerned with iconography, chant, dream and relational presence. These works adapt inherited forms experimentally and are presented as personal constructions rather than recovered historical systems.

The terracotta marque adapts a detail from the Processional Cross of Herat.
