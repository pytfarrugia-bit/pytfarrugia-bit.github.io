Bio

Peter Marc Farrugia

Warranted Gestalt psychotherapist, theological researcher and writer from Malta.

Peter Marc Farrugia is a warranted Gestalt psychotherapist in Malta. He practises clinically with adults and older adolescents, offering in-person and online work in English and Maltese. His approach is relational and trauma-informed, with particular attention to bodily experience, shame, meaning and the relation between psychological and spiritual life.

Alongside clinical practice, he mentors psychotherapy students during placement and works as a visiting lecturer, trainer and workshop facilitator. His professional work also includes safeguarding, victim care and the development of resources concerned with trauma, institutional response and the conditions under which care remains trustworthy.

He is undertaking doctoral research in theology at St Mary’s University, Twickenham. His thesis considers Holy Saturday as a theological account of wounded time and the limits of closure after harm, with particular attention to the form of presence Mary keeps before the sealed place. His wider academic writing ranges across Christian theology, psychotherapy, safeguarding and material religious practice.

He also develops visual, musical and devotional projects concerned with iconography, chant, dream and memory, adapting inherited forms experientially rather than presenting them as recovered historical systems.

Across his clinical, theological and artistic work, he is concerned with how experience is carried after harm, how meaning is formed and how presence can be maintained without forcing premature resolution.

Pietru is the Maltese form of Peter and the name used by his grandparents. His drawings and exhibitions are signed with it.

The terracotta marque adapts a detail from the Processional Cross of Herat.
