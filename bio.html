Bio

Peter Marc Farrugia

Warranted Gestalt psychotherapist, lecturer, and theological researcher from Malta.

Peter Marc Farrugia is a warranted Gestalt psychotherapist in Malta. He works with adults, in person and online, in English and Maltese. His practice is relational and trauma-informed, attending to bodily experience, shame, meaning, and the ways overwhelming experience may continue in relationships and everyday life.

He also mentors psychotherapy students on placement and works as a visiting lecturer, trainer, and workshop facilitator. His professional work includes safeguarding and victim care, with particular interest in the conditions under which care remains trustworthy.

He is undertaking doctoral research in theology at St Mary’s University, Twickenham. His thesis considers Holy Saturday as an account of wounded time and the limits of closure after harm. His wider writing brings psychotherapy into conversation with Christian theology, safeguarding, and material religious practice.

He also develops projects in image, music, and devotion, working with iconography, chant, dream, and memory. These projects adapt inherited forms experientially rather than presenting them as recovered historical systems.

Pietru is the Maltese form of Peter and the name used by his grandparents. His drawings and exhibitions are signed with it.

The terracotta marque develops the inner negative spaces of the Processional Cross of Herat rather than reproducing its outline. Its paired curves retain something of the cross’s hooked ornament, while the central opening and broken crown recall the damaged upper part of the surviving object.
