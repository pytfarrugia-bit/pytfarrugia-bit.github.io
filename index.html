Gestalt psychotherapy in Malta and online

Psychotherapy after traumatic experience

I work with adults recovering from traumatic or overwhelming experiences, including the ways these may appear as anxiety, depressive tendencies, shame, withdrawal, bodily distress, or difficulty feeling safe. Sessions are offered in English or Maltese, in person in Malta or online.

Practice

A careful pace after overwhelming experience

My approach is Gestalt and trauma-informed. I attend to how experience appears in the body, in relationships, and in the present encounter. The work is not to force disclosure or hurry towards resolution, but to find a pace at which difficult experience can be approached without becoming overwhelming.
