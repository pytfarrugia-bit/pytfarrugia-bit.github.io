Peter Marc Farrugia

Peter Marc Farrugia works on what remains open after a matter has been closed, in clinical practice, in theology and in drawing.

Practice

Psychotherapy in Malta and online

Peter offers individual psychotherapy to adults and older adolescents who may be dealing with anxiety, trauma, abuse, relationship difficulties, grief, shame or a period of change. His approach is Gestalt psychotherapy informed by trauma theory, with attention to present experience, the body and patterns of contact. Sessions are available in English or Maltese.

Sessions last 50 minutes and are offered in person at O’Hea Pharmacy in Gżira or online by Zoom. He is currently accepting a limited number of new clients. The fee is €40; an in-person appointment also carries a €5 clinic fee. A limited concession may sometimes be agreed where the full fee would prevent someone from beginning.

Peter holds Malta psychotherapy warrant no. 314. In Maltese law, “warranted” means statutorily authorised to practise as a psychotherapist. He is not currently registered with a UK professional regulator.
