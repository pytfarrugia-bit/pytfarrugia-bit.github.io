Practice

A relational and trauma-informed approach

My work is grounded in Gestalt psychotherapy. It attends to present experience, bodily responses, relationships and the meanings people form around what has happened to them. I do not assume that distress can be reduced to a symptom or hurried towards explanation. The pace of the work is considered carefully, particularly where trauma, shame or experiences of being overwhelmed are involved.

Religious belief, spiritual experience, doubt and conflict may enter therapy when they matter to the client. They are approached as part of the person’s experience, without requiring any particular belief or practice.
