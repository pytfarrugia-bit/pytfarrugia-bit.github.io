Psychotherapy

About the practitioner

Peter Marc Farrugia is a warranted Gestalt psychotherapist working with adults in English and Maltese, in person in Malta and online.

He completed postgraduate training in Gestalt psychotherapy and holds a warrant registered with the Psychotherapy Profession Board in Malta. His work is relational and trauma-informed, attending to bodily experience, shame, meaning and the ways overwhelming experience may continue in relationships and everyday life.

His professional experience also includes safeguarding and victim care, psychotherapy student mentoring, teaching and training. Clinical work is undertaken within professional supervision.

Scope of the practice

The practice is for adults living with the effects of traumatic or overwhelming experience, including harm within relationships, families, communities or institutions. Religious or spiritual questions may be explored when they matter to the client; psychotherapy is not spiritual direction or religious formation.

The practice is not an emergency service and cannot provide continuous monitoring or immediate crisis intervention.
