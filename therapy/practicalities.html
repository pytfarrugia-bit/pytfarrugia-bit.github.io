Practicalities and professional standing

The practical and accountability arrangements for the practice are set out here so that they do not have to be negotiated without a clear starting point.

Sessions

Format
In person in Malta or online
Languages
English and Maltese
Session length
Generally 50 minutes
Fee
€40 per session. A reduced fee is available where cost would otherwise prevent the work; please say so when you write.
Cancellation
At least 24 hours’ notice is required; otherwise the session is normally charged.
Availability
Currently accepting enquiries

Confidentiality

Sessions are confidential within the ethical and legal limits explained before therapy begins. These limits include circumstances in which there is a serious concern about immediate harm or a legal obligation to disclose information.

Verification and accountability

Warrant

Gestalt Psychotherapist, warrant no. 314, issued by the Psychotherapy Profession Board in Malta on 16 September 2022. View the official register.

Professional membership

Member of the Malta Association for Psychotherapy (MAP) and Friend of the Gestalt Psychotherapy Training Institute (GPTI), UK.

Contact-form data

The enquiry form collects the name, email address and message supplied by the sender and transmits them through Formspree. Do not include detailed clinical information at this stage.

The practice pages and contact form cannot provide emergency or crisis support.

If you need urgent support, Supportline 179 is free and available 24 hours; Kellimni.com offers free online support. Call 112 where there is immediate danger.
